FreeSpeech is an open-source AI voice assistant for Android Auto designed for privacy-respecting Android ROMs — Murena /e/OS, GrapheneOS, CalyxOS, LineageOS — where Google Assistant is absent and the car microphone button shows "speech commands are currently not supported".

FreeSpeech fills that gap without sending your voice to Google. All processing runs on your own self-hosted servers.

<b>How it works</b>

1. Tap the FreeSpeech icon on the Android Auto car screen (or say the wake word)
2. Speak your command — a voice-activity detector stops recording on silence
3. Your audio is transcribed by your Whisper server (self-hosted, on your hardware)
4. An LLM (Ollama/Qwen locally, or Gemini/OpenAI/Anthropic via API) classifies your intent
5. FreeSpeech opens the right app or speaks a TTS answer through the car speakers

<b>Supported commands</b>

• Music — opens Tidal, Spotify, YouTube Music with your search
• Navigation — opens Google Maps, OsmAnd, Here WeGo
• Phone calls — opens the dialler
• Weather, web search — opens your preferred apps
• Custom topics — map any phrase to any app or site-restricted search
• RAG documents — answer questions from your own PDFs or EPUBs (travel guides, manuals, …)
• Conversational — informational answers spoken via TTS through the car speaker (ducks music)

<b>Privacy</b>

• No data leaves your network — Whisper, the LLM, and TTS all run on your own hardware
• No Google account, no Google Assistant, no Play Services required
• Audio recordings are kept locally for debugging and can be disabled

<b>Requirements</b>

• A self-hosted Whisper server (faster-whisper, whisper.cpp, or compatible)
• A self-hosted LLM via Ollama, or an API key for Gemini / OpenAI / Anthropic
• The FreeSpeech Console (Rails backend — included in the GitHub repository)
• Android Auto (wireless or USB)
